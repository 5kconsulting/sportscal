Good to know

Do their existing calendars come with them?

Yes. If your co-parent already had a SportsCal account with kids or feeds set up, everything they had comes along when they accept — and you'll both see it right after.

Can I invite more than one person?

Household size is capped at two for now — the common co-parent case. If you'd use a third seat (grandparent, nanny), tell us — that's the fastest way to move it up the list.

What if they never respond?

The invite expires after 7 days. You can also cancel it any time from the Household section — the magic link stops working immediately when you do.

Can we un-join later?

Either of you can leave from the Household section. The one who leaves keeps their own kids and calendars — they just go back to their own solo view.
