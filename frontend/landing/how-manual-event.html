Add a manual event

A birthday party, a dentist appointment, a family scrimmage — anything not in a team app that still needs to be on the family calendar. Four short steps and you're done.

Manual events show up on the Dashboard right next to synced events and push to Apple Calendar through the same feed.

On the Dashboard right away

Manual events live on your Dashboard mixed in with automatically-synced ones, and get pushed to Apple Calendar through the same feed URL. Edit or delete anytime by tapping the event.

When to add manually
🎂
Birthday parties
A friend's party, a family gathering, anything that would clash with a practice you should reschedule.
🛡
Doctor / dentist
Appointments that need to be blocked out on the family calendar — and shared with a co-parent handling pickups.
⚽
Backyard scrimmage
A pickup game or one-off practice the coach organizes over text, not through the team app.
📌
Anything not in an app
Grandparent visits, school pickup swap days — if you'd write it on the fridge, add it here.
Have a single team calendar that covers multiple kids? See Route events to the right kid — SportsCal can split them by age group with a text pattern.
