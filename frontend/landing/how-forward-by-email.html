Forward events by email

Got a work meeting, a school district calendar, or a coach's email with practice times? Forward it to your unique SportsCal address and the events show up on your Dashboard — no copy-paste, no re-typing.

Best for events that live outside your kids' sports apps — work calendars, school district newsletters, one-off invites — that still need to show up next to the family schedule.

What SportsCal can pull from an email
📧
iCal / webcal links
Any webcal:// or .ics URL in the email body — typed, hyperlinked, or in a signature. SportsCal follows the link and pulls every event on it.
📎
Meeting invites (.ics)
A calendar invite attached to a meeting notification. Read directly — no separate subscription needed for one-off events.
🎓
School district emails
Field trip dates, early dismissal, teacher planning days. Most district newsletters carry a subscribe link at the bottom.
🏇
Coach's group email
The Sunday email with the practice schedule for the week — forward it and SportsCal grabs anything schedule-shaped.
One-way and private to you
🔒
Address is unique to your account
Every account gets a different token. Even if someone else emails your address, only your calendar sees the events. Rotate the address anytime from Settings.
📧
Forward from any email you own
Home Gmail, work Outlook, school district webmail — SportsCal doesn't care which sender you forward from, only which address you forward to.
For a printed schedule with no email attached, use Snap a schedule photo instead — it uses the same forwarding address for the file.
