Got a paper handout from the coach or a screenshot of a schedule? Take a photo. SportsCal reads the dates, times, and locations and puts the events on your Dashboard for you.

Events on your Dashboard

Same as the paste-a-URL flow — events show up on your family Dashboard, colored by kid. Photo-added events don't re-sync automatically (there's no calendar to poll), so if the coach reschedules, snap a new photo.

What SportsCal can read
📜
Paper schedules
The typed handout the coach gave out at the first practice, printed on regular paper. Hold your phone steady and make sure the words are readable.
📱
Screenshots
A screenshot of a webpage, a group text, or a PDF viewer works too — pick "Photo Library" instead of the camera, then choose the screenshot.
📋
Whiteboards
Handwritten whiteboards work if the writing is neat and legible. Chicken scratch is a stretch — ask the coach for the typed version if it comes out wrong.
✕
Doesn't work
Blurry photos, extreme angles, poor lighting, and screenshots so tiny the text is a smudge. Give the picture a quick look before hitting Use photo.
