Route events to the right kid

Some club apps send every age group down one calendar feed. Give each kid a short text pattern that matches their team's events, and SportsCal routes each event to the right kid automatically.

Show up in club soccer (BU12/GU10 combined feeds), club basketball (10U/12U mixed calendars), and TeamSnap One family accounts.

Each kid sees only their own events

Filter the Dashboard to Riley and only 10U events show up. Filter to Sam, only 12U events. Their personal iCal feeds (from Share a kid's calendar) are also filtered, so their Apple Calendar stays clean.

Common pattern examples
10U
Age groups
"10U", "U10", "10-under" — whatever your club uses. Type the exact string that appears in the event title.
A
Team letter or level
"A Team", "JV", "Varsity", "Blue Team". Pick something unique to that squad — a color, a letter, whatever the league uses.
GU
Boys / girls indicators
"GU12" for girls under 12, "BU12" for boys. Common in club soccer feeds that mix both.
?
Safety fallback
Some events don't match any pattern (an all-club scrimmage, say). SportsCal shows those to every kid on the source so you don't miss anything — and you can tighten the pattern later.
