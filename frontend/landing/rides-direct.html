Three ways to notify
📧
Email

Sends a formatted email with Yes and No tap-buttons. Same rule as the group flow — first tap wins.

Best when · you have their email but not their phone
💬
Open Messages

Opens iMessage with the request pre-filled for one recipient. Parent can tweak the text before sending — good for a personal note.

Best when · you want to say something extra
📝
Just assign

Marks the person as assigned on the event. No email, no message, no ping. They won't know unless you tell them separately.

Best when · you already talked to them
Yes and No taps come back to SportsCal — same one-driver-per-ride rule as the group flow.
