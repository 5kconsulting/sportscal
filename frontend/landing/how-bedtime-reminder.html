Bedtime reminder

Every night at 8 PM, a push notification lands on your lock screen listing tomorrow's events. Enough time to pack cleats, wash uniforms, and set the right alarms.

Best for the family member who does the "what does everyone need tomorrow?" math the night before — and would rather have it handed to them.

Toggle the reminder on

Under Settings → Notifications, flip Bedtime push on. First tap triggers the iOS permission prompt — you have to say yes there too.

STEP 2 · iOS PERMISSION
SportsCal dimmed behind the prompt…
"SportsCal" Would Like to Send You Notifications
Notifications may include alerts, sounds, and icon badges. You can configure these in Settings.
Tap

Tap Allow

Standard iOS permission ask — one time only per install. Tap Allow and SportsCal is greenlit to send you the bedtime push (and nothing else, unless you turn on more).

A note on timezones
🌐
Local time, not UTC
SportsCal fires at 8 PM in your local time. Traveling east or west doesn't shift when it lands.
😴
Nothing tomorrow? Nothing arrives.
Empty schedule tomorrow means no push. Your evening stays quiet.
Prefer a broader look at the whole week? Weekly digest email lands Friday mornings.
