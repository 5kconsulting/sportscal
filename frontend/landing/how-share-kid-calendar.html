SportsCal · Guides

Share a kid's calendar

Every kid gets their own subscribable URL. AirDrop it once, and their SportsCal schedule lives in their Apple Calendar — no app to install on their phone.

Works for kids old enough to have their own phone, second parents, grandparents, nannies, or a coach who wants the kid's schedule.
