# App Store listing — proposed copy (2026-11-13)

Why: the store name and subtitle read like an app for following teams, and a third
of the people who got stuck in setup were looking for NFL or college schedules.
This copy says "kids" up front, drops "iCal", and only claims features that exist.

Name, subtitle, description and screenshots can only change with a new app version.
Promotional text can be changed any time without a new version.

## Description
```
SportsCal is for parents with kids on sports teams. It puts every practice, game and meet from your kids' team apps into one family calendar, and tells you when two kids need to be in two places at once.

(It is not for following pro or college teams. It doesn't carry NFL, NBA or college schedules.)

GET YOUR FIRST TEAM IN ABOUT A MINUTE
• Pick your team app and we show you exactly where its calendar link is
• Paste the link and the whole season appears. It updates on its own when the coach changes something
• Works with TeamSnap, GameChanger, PlayMetrics, SportsEngine, BYGA, TeamSideline, LeagueApps and more
• No team app? Take a photo or screenshot of the schedule and SportsCal reads it
• Got the schedule as a PDF or an email? Forward it to your private SportsCal address

ONE CALENDAR FOR THE WHOLE FAMILY
• Every kid in their own color, so you can see who is where at a glance
• Conflicts flagged: two kids, two places, same time
• Shows up in Apple Calendar, Google Calendar or Outlook if you want it there
• Each kid gets a calendar link you can share with grandparents or a sitter
• Add your co-parent and you both see the same thing

SORT OUT THE RIDES
• Ask one person, or text the whole team at once. The first yes gets the ride
• Mark a kid as not going without changing the team's schedule

KNOW TOMORROW, TONIGHT
• An 8 PM heads-up with tomorrow's schedule
• Add one-off events yourself, including ones that repeat every week

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
• Your family's data is not sold or shared
• SportsCal only reads your team calendars. It never posts anything back

Free for 2 kids and 2 calendars. Premium adds more of both, plus ride requests and a weekly email digest.

Built by a dad of four with kids in two sports apiece, because juggling a dozen schedules across five apps every week was making me crazy.

Terms of Use (EULA): https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/
```
